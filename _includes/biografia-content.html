I´m Mauricio

a wedding photographer based in Switzerland

For more than 11 years, I have been capturing meaningful moments in people's lives, always with a calm, organized and natural approach.

I believe great wedding photography is not only about beautiful images, but about trust, planning and emotional connection.

Every couple is different. That's why I take time to understand who you are, what matters to you, and how you want to experience your wedding day.

My goal is simple: to help you enjoy your day without stress, while I document it in an authentic and timeless way.

I was born in Colombia and moved to Europe in 2001.

I studied Art Photography in the Canary Islands and later worked as a freelance photographer in Madrid, collaborating with international media and artists.

Today, I am based in Bern and available throughout Switzerland and Europe.

I work with couples who value natural moments, emotional storytelling and a well-planned wedding experience.

It would be an honor to document your story and create images that you will relive with the same emotion, even many years from now.
